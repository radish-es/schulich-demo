At the Schulich School of Law, our research is uniquely framed by the Weldon tradition of unselfish pursuit of public service, grounded in our commitment to community at multiple, overlapping scales. We aim to push boundaries, make change, and create genuine impact led by community needs. At the same time, our research is anchored in a commitment to nourish and sustain our impact. At times, this means resisting pressures to chase the next big project, react to the next crisis, or respond to each and every demand of the moment. Our willingness and capacity to confront this challenge and maintain our balance sets the Schulich School of Law of apart among institutions for legal and policy research across Canada and around the world.
